Animal lineup

Local sign-off of the animal meshes (docs/animal-library.md). Each row: the drawn mesh (left) and the procedural model (right) at true size on neutral ground, a 1 m pole and ground bar in 10 cm bands, under the game's sun, sky light and exposure for the season and hour. Drag a view to turn it. Measurements are taken from the geometry at rest; the brief's numbers come from library/animals/<id>.json and library/animals/drafts/. Red marks a difference of more than 10%. A sign-off stays in this browser; copy JSON and paste it into an issue.
